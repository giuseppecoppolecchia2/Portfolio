# Profile portrait

`giuseppe-coppolecchia.webp` is Giuseppe Coppolecchia's own profile photograph, supplied as `Linkedin-Photo-compressa.jpg` and authorized for this portfolio on 2 November 2026.

The original was resized to 768×768 and encoded as WebP (quality 84, 56,534 bytes), without retaining EXIF metadata. The original photo remains unchanged. The responsive crop is applied through CSS: 4:5 on desktop, square on mobile. No generative editing was used.
